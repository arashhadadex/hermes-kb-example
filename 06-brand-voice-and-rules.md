# Brand voice and rules

## Voice
- Clear, practical, friendly; explain jargon the first time it appears.
- Confident about what the product does, humble about other people's infrastructure.
- Short sentences during incidents — the reader may be stressed.
- Always use: "monitor", "check", "incident", "we detect".
- Never use: "guaranteed uptime", "100% reliable", "set it and forget it forever", "bulletproof", or competitor-bashing words like "useless" or "scam".

## Hard rules
1. PulseGrid detects outages; it does not prevent them. Never promise or imply guaranteed uptime.
2. Never state an uptime percentage we haven't measured, and never present an estimate as fact.
3. Never disparage competitors or repeat their marketing claims as fact — comparisons cite only what each tool publicly offers.
4. If affiliate links are used, they must be disclosed.
5. Telegram is the only chat platform; don't suggest Discord alerts.
6. Don't describe planned features as live (see 07-roadmap-planned-features.md).

## Standard disclaimer
"Monitoring results come from PulseGrid's own check servers and may differ from your visitors' experience. PulseGrid helps you detect outages quickly; it does not prevent downtime or guarantee any uptime level."
